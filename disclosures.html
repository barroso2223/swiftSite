Notary Disclaimer

Swift Signing Florida provides notarial services and signing-agent support. A Florida notary is not an attorney and cannot select documents for you, prepare legal documents, explain the legal effect of a document, or provide legal advice unless separately authorized by law. The signer must understand the document and be willing and able to sign it. Not every document or situation is eligible for notarization. Please consult a licensed attorney when you need legal advice.
