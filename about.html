About Swift Signing Florida

Dependable Service for Florida Families

Swift Signing Florida was founded by Daniel Barroso to make important signings and life insurance decisions feel clearer, more convenient, and less overwhelming.

My Story

I'm Daniel Barroso, the founder of Swift Signing Florida. Since 2019, I have helped individuals, families, title companies, and other professionals complete important signings with convenient mobile notary service throughout South Florida.

My professional background also includes service in the U.S. Army, Florida real estate, and licensed life insurance work. Although these services are different, they share one purpose: helping people navigate some of life's most important decisions with clarity and confidence.

As a husband and father of two young children, I understand that decisions involving your home, family, and financial protection are personal. My goal is never to pressure or rush you. Whether I'm meeting you for a real estate closing or helping you understand life insurance options, I want you to feel informed, comfortable, and confident about your next step.
