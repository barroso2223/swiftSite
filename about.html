About Swift Signing Florida

Making Important Decisions Feel Simpler.

Swift Signing Florida was founded by Daniel Barroso with a simple goal: make important signings and life insurance decisions easier to understand, more convenient, and less stressful. Whether you're purchasing life insurance or scheduling a mobile notary appointment, you'll receive clear communication, honest guidance, and personalized service every step of the way.

My Story

I'm Daniel Barroso, founder of Swift Signing Florida. Since 2019, I've helped individuals, families, title companies, lenders, and real estate professionals complete important signings with reliable mobile notary service throughout South Florida.

Over the years, my career has expanded beyond notary work. Today, I'm also a licensed life insurance professional and Florida real estate professional. While these services are different, they all share the same purpose: helping people make informed decisions during some of life's biggest moments.

As a U.S. Army veteran, I believe in serving others with integrity, professionalism, and respect. Whether I'm meeting you at a closing table or helping you understand your life insurance options, my goal is the same—to provide clear information, honest guidance, and a smooth experience without pressure.

I'm also a husband and father of two young children, so I understand how important it is to protect your family, your home, and your future. I believe every client deserves someone who takes the time to answer questions, explain options clearly, and make the process feel less overwhelming.

When you work with Swift Signing Florida, you're working directly with me—not a large call center or a team of rotating agents. I appreciate the opportunity to earn your trust and help you move forward with confidence.
