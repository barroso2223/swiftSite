Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Swift Signing Florida collects through this website, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Effective date: August 4, 2026

Last updated: August 4, 2026

1. Who We Are

Swift Signing Florida provides mobile notary services and general life insurance education and assistance. In this policy, “Swift Signing Florida,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to Swift Signing Florida and Daniel Barroso.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide through our website forms, email, telephone, or text communications. Depending on the form or service requested, this may include:

  • Your name, email address, and telephone number
  • Your preferred contact method
  • Information about a requested notary appointment, such as document category, preferred location, and requested date or time
  • General information about your life insurance questions, goals, state of residence, relationship to a proposed insured, or approximate budget
  • A child's age or date of birth when submitted by a parent, legal guardian, grandparent, or other authorized adult requesting information about a child's policy
  • Any other information you choose to include in a message
Please do not submit sensitive information through general forms. Do not enter Social Security numbers, bank or payment-card details, driver's-license images, detailed medical records, account credentials, or other highly sensitive information. When sensitive information is needed for an insurance application or another transaction, it should be provided through an appropriate secure carrier, vendor, or direct process.

3. Information Collected Automatically

Our hosting provider and other technology providers may automatically receive limited technical information when you visit the site, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, requested pages, timestamps, and security or diagnostic data. We may also receive aggregate website-performance information if analytics tools are enabled.

This website does not currently claim to use advertising cookies or sell personal information. This policy should be updated before adding analytics, advertising pixels, behavioral advertising, or additional tracking technologies.

4. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Respond to questions and service requests
  • Schedule or discuss mobile notary appointments
  • Provide general life insurance information and follow up about a quote, illustration, application, or policy-related inquiry
  • Send service-related confirmations and communications
  • Maintain business records and document customer communications
  • Protect the website, prevent fraud or spam, and troubleshoot problems
  • Comply with applicable legal, regulatory, licensing, and contractual obligations

We do not use a general website inquiry as authorization to issue coverage. Insurance coverage is subject to a separate application, carrier review, underwriting, policy issuance, acceptance, and payment requirements.

5. Service Providers and Third Parties

We may disclose information to service providers that help operate the website or fulfill a request, only as reasonably necessary for those services. These may include:

  • Netlify: website hosting, form processing, spam filtering, submission storage, and form notifications
  • Insurance carriers and authorized insurance platforms: when you choose to request a quote, illustration, application, or policy service
  • Email, telephone, and communications providers: to respond to and communicate about your request
  • YouTube/Google: when an embedded YouTube video is displayed or played on a blog or resource page
  • Professional advisers or authorities: when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights, investigate fraud, or respond to lawful requests

External websites and platforms operate under their own privacy policies. Review those policies before submitting information through an external service.

6. YouTube Videos and External Links

Blog posts may include YouTube videos using YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed domain. Google or YouTube may still receive information when you interact with an embedded player. The site also contains links to third-party insurance application platforms and other websites. We do not control the privacy, security, availability, or content of those third-party services.

7. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. Children should not submit information through this website. Information concerning a child's insurance request must be submitted by a parent, legal guardian, grandparent, or other authorized adult.

Contact us if you believe a child under 13 submitted personal information directly so that we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

8. Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to maintain appropriate business and compliance records, to resolve disputes, and to satisfy legal, regulatory, licensing, contractual, or carrier requirements. Retention periods may differ by record type.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information. The site uses HTTPS when properly deployed. However, no website, email, form, storage system, or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Your Choices and Requests

Subject to applicable law and recordkeeping obligations, you may ask us to review, correct, or delete personal information that you submitted through this website. You may also ask us to stop nonessential follow-up communications.

Send requests to swiftsigningfl@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

11. Do Not Track and Sale of Information

We do not currently sell personal information for monetary consideration. Because there is not yet a universally accepted standard for browser “Do Not Track” signals, this website does not currently respond to those signals. Update this section if the site's data practices change.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy as our website, services, vendors, or legal obligations change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
